### TASK2 - Velocity_selector
Your starting point is similar the solution from TASK1, provided here
as [Exercise_vselector.instr](Exercise_vselector.instr).

Your taks is to replace the chopper with a velocity selector for transport of
wavelengths around `L_target`.

### HINTS
* Use `mcdoc V_selector` to understand the component parameters
* Fill in the expression for `vselector_angular_frequency`
* You may (mostly) use the default values coming from D11@ILL
* You will have to make more room for the velocity selector than for
  the chopper as the housing is bigger

You _may_ cheat by looking at the solution file
